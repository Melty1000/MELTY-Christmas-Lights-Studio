# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Melty's Christmas Lights Studio serves streamers, OBS users, Streamer.bot users, Melty power users, and first-time creators who want a high-quality holiday lighting overlay without fighting the setup. Users may arrive with very different confidence levels: some will know OBS browser sources and presets well, while others will need the product to guide them through the first usable setup.

The primary workflow is configuring a live visual overlay before or during stream preparation. The interface should support simple setup, confident exploration, detailed customization, preset-driven reuse, and future Streamer.bot-powered automation without making the product feel intimidating.

## Product Purpose

The product lets creators build, preview, save, export, and run customizable Christmas lights as an OBS-ready browser overlay. Success means a first-time user can get a good-looking overlay running quickly, then continue discovering deeper controls as their needs grow.

The long-term direction is a creator tool where presets and Streamer.bot integration carry more of the live workflow, reducing the need for fast manual tweaking while still preserving advanced creative control. Visual output quality and runtime performance are non-negotiable because the tool directly affects the stream.

## Brand Personality

Fun, premium, creator-first.

The product should feel expressive and enjoyable without becoming a toy. It should feel polished and capable without becoming sterile. The voice can be playful and personal, but controls, layout, and feedback should respect that users are configuring something that will be seen live by an audience.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, sterile admin panels, outdated OBS plugin interfaces, overly cute holiday toy styling, cluttered novelty controls, and visual treatments that make the UI look heavier than the overlay it controls.

Avoid designs where personality comes at the cost of clarity, performance, or control density. Avoid making advanced users dig through decorative surfaces to reach real settings.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with first-run clarity. The first useful result should be obvious, quick, and confidence-building.
2. Reveal depth without dumping complexity. Advanced customization should be available, organized, and progressively discoverable.
3. Make the output feel premium. The UI exists to help creators produce stream visuals that look intentional and polished.
4. Keep the studio creator-first. Prioritize OBS, Streamer.bot, presets, previewing, saving, and export workflows over generic app patterns.
5. Protect performance. UI polish, animation, preview rendering, and deep controls must not compromise smooth interaction or stream-safe rendering.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, keyboard-usable controls, visible focus states, clear labels, and state changes that do not rely on color alone. Support reduced motion for UI-only animation where possible. Keep dense controls legible and predictable for users who are configuring the overlay inside OBS interaction windows or browser windows of varying sizes.
